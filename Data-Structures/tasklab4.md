Task 1 — Build and Display Both Ways
Create a doubly linked list of 5 subject names using insert_at_tail. Display it forward and then backward.
Task 2 — Insert at Head and Tail
Insert one new subject at the head and another at the tail. Display the list in both directions after each insertion.
Task 3 — Delete a Node
Delete a subject from the middle of the list, then delete the head subject. Display the list in both directions after
each deletion and confirm they are still mirror images.
Task 4 — Circular List
Build a circular linked list of 4 names and display it. Then write a loop that starts at head and prints exactly 10
consecutive nodes by following next (without stopping at head) to prove the list wraps around.
Task 5 — Conceptual (No Coding)
(a) In 3-4 sentences, explain why deleting a node you already hold a reference to is easier in a doubly linked
list than in a singly linked list. (b) Give one real-world situation where a circular linked list is a natural fit and say
why.
